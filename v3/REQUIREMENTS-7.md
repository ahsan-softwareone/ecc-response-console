# Round 8: Incident Overview

Summary of the request. See README, "Round 8 changes", for what was built.

- The Incident Manager lands on an Incident Overview for the active incident; other tabs stay visible; a calm empty state when there is none. Opening it is read-only; command transfer stays manual.
- Five sections: Incident Details (with incident-level severity, or "Severity not yet assessed"), Current Situation Summary (as of a time, sources distinguished, evidence linked, stale or conflicting information visible), Requires Attention (what, why, what is uncertain, owner if recorded, one action, View all), Sequence of Events (turning points, expandable, event and received times) and Summary for Exec (copyable, same record and time).
- Layout: details across the top, summary and attention side by side where space allows, then sequence and exec. Detail one click away in Response, Observations, Sources, the viewer and Sit. Rep; returning restores the manager's place.
- Demo: D-0029 fire containment reported by the field, verification pending, distinct from D-0001 pressure falling below its illustrative High limit, with only evidence available at the current stage.
- Verify in both themes at 1440x900 and 1366x768, including the no-incident and not-assessed states and unchanged workflow state.
