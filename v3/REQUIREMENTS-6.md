# Round 7: Investigation workspace follow-through

Summary of the request. See README, "Round 7 changes", for what was built.

- Response, Observations and Sources are connected views of one selected alert, observation or investigation, with a compact context label; scope is labelled and no unrelated response appears as an answer.
- User observations get a separate analysis status and an outcome summary, with links to investigation, assessment and recommendation that persist. Assessed does not mean verified, accepted, resolved or safe.
- "its going to blow" is the ambiguous, urgent case: preserved wording, a short clarification in the Assistant, current evidence that does not dismiss it, urgency separate from certainty, nothing invented.
- Response explains the next step and keeps recommendation, decision, reported execution and observed outcome distinct.
- Sources defaults to evidence used, including human reports and readings, says what each supports, and separates other material.
- Paused or stopped agent: saved and pending. Failed analysis: preserved, retry without duplicates. Everything persists in the record, timeline, history and exports. The Situation Ask / Report button is removed.
